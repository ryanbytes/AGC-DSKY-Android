AGC DSKY v1.1.66

- PHONE CLOCK now uses YEAR, MONTH and DAY labels in the original Apollo DSKY label typography and position, with a two-digit year/month/day.
- Adds a DREAM DATE option for date-labelled clock presentation in Android Dream Mode (defaults off).
- Normal AGC restores the original PROG, VERB and NOUN labels.
- Keeps hours, minutes and seconds in the three existing clock registers.

Regular and Fire APKs use the established standalone release signer, not a CI signing identity.
