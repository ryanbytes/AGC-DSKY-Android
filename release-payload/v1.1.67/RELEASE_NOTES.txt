AGC DSKY v1.1.67

- Adds two separate Android home widgets: DSKY Classic Clock and DSKY Date Clock.
- Existing widgets retain their provider and become Classic DSKY Clock.
- Classic shows PROG / VERB / NOUN with HH/MM/SS registers.
- Date shows YEAR / MONTH / DAY with YY/MM/DD and HH/MM/SS registers.
- Both widgets run independently of the app mode and Dream mode.
- Original standalone-signed Regular and Fire APK payloads; physical device widgets not yet tested.
