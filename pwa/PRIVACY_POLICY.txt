PRIVACY POLICY — AGC DSKY WEB APP (PWA)
Effective date: September 9, 2026

AGC DSKY is an independent historical Apollo Guidance Computer / DSKY simulator.

DATA ACCESSED BY SIMULATOR FEATURES
- Camera: may be used for a live sextant/optics view when enabled and permitted by the browser.
- Approximate or precise location: may be used for local astronomical calculations such as horizon/navigation-star assistance and local sunrise/sunset brightness control when enabled and permitted by the browser.
- Motion and orientation sensor data: may be used for simulated spacecraft/optics input paths when enabled and permitted by the browser.
- Ambient light sensor: when the browser exposes it, local illuminance readings may be used only on-device to adjust DSKY display brightness. Ambient-light readings are not sent to the analytics service.
- App state and preferences: simulator state, settings, brightness mode, cached solar coordinates, and related configuration may be stored locally so the app can restore its state.

ANONYMOUS USAGE ANALYTICS
The PWA may send a small anonymous usage event to a developer-operated analytics endpoint when analytics are enabled for the deployed site. The event contains only:
- launch or installation event type;
- a random installation identifier generated and stored locally by the browser;
- whether the app is running in standalone/Home Screen mode;
- a coarse device class such as iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows, Linux, or other;
- the app build identifier.

The analytics backend HMAC-hashes the random installation identifier before storing it. The raw installation identifier is not stored by the backend. The backend does not intentionally read or store the request IP address, exact location, browser history, camera data, ambient-light readings, motion/orientation sensor data, AGC inputs, simulator state, names, email addresses, or user-account information.

The aggregate statistics endpoint exposes counts only, such as total anonymous installations/users, launches, Home Screen users, recent active users, and coarse device totals. It does not expose per-user records.

Cloudflare provides the Worker and D1 infrastructure used by the optional analytics backend and necessarily processes network requests to provide that service. The application does not use advertising analytics or sell analytics data.

USER CONTROL
Do Not Track is honored by the PWA analytics client. Analytics can also be disabled locally by opening the PWA with ?telemetry=off. Opening it with ?telemetry=on removes that local opt-out. Clearing the site's browser storage removes the locally stored random installation identifier, simulator preferences, and cached solar location.

OFFLINE USE
After the PWA has cached its required files, the AGC simulator can run offline. Cached coordinates can continue to provide sunrise/sunset dimming calculations while offline. Analytics events cannot be transmitted while offline; failure to transmit analytics does not prevent simulator operation.

RETENTION AND DELETION
Simulator state, settings, cached solar coordinates, and the random analytics installation identifier may remain in browser storage until cleared by the user. Ambient-light readings are kept only in memory while the app runs. Aggregate analytics and HMAC-hashed anonymous installation records may be retained by the developer for usage measurement. Because the backend does not store the raw random identifier or an account identity, it is not designed to associate an analytics record with a named individual.

CHILDREN
The app is a general historical/technical simulator and is not specifically directed to children. It does not knowingly collect names, contact information, precise location, or other identifying profile information from children or other users through the analytics feature.
