PRIVACY POLICY — AGC DSKY WEB APP (PWA)
Effective date: September 9, 2026

AGC DSKY is an independent historical Apollo Guidance Computer / DSKY simulator.

DATA ACCESSED BY SIMULATOR FEATURES
- Camera: may be used for a live sextant/optics view when enabled and permitted by the browser.
- Approximate or precise location: may be used for local astronomical calculations such as horizon/navigation-star assistance and local sunrise/sunset brightness control when enabled and permitted by the browser.
- Motion and orientation sensor data: may be used for simulated spacecraft/optics input paths when enabled and permitted by the browser.
- Ambient light sensor: when the browser exposes it, local illuminance readings may be used only on-device to adjust DSKY display brightness.
- App state and preferences: simulator state, settings, brightness mode, cached solar coordinates, and related configuration may be stored locally so the app can restore its state.

ANALYTICS AND NETWORK USE
The PWA does not include analytics or send usage events. It does not create an analytics installation identifier or send AGC inputs, simulator state, camera data, or sensor readings to an analytics service. Browser network requests are limited by the app's Content Security Policy to the same origin for packaged resources and the HTTP-Date clock fallback. Browser and hosting services may process ordinary connection metadata, including the device IP address, to serve those requests.

USER CONTROL
Clearing the site's browser storage removes locally saved simulator preferences and cached solar location. The PWA does not store an analytics installation identifier.

OFFLINE USE
After the PWA has cached its required files, the AGC simulator can run offline. Cached coordinates can continue to provide sunrise/sunset dimming calculations while offline. When online, the same-origin HTTP-Date fallback may request the site's manifest headers for clock correction.

RETENTION AND DELETION
Simulator state, settings, and cached solar coordinates may remain in browser storage until cleared by the user. Ambient-light readings are kept only in memory while the app runs. The PWA does not retain analytics records.

CHILDREN
The app is a general historical/technical simulator and is not specifically directed to children. The PWA does not include an analytics feature or knowingly collect names, contact information, or user profiles.
